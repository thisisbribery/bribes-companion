Set up the Deluge (once)
  1. SETTINGS > MIDI > MIDI-FOLLOW > CHANNEL A: set it to the MIDI Follow channel above.
  2. SETTINGS > MIDI > MIDI-FOLLOW > FEEDBACK > CHANNEL: set it to A, so the knobs here show the Deluge's values.
  3. Keep SETTINGS > MIDI > CLOCK > OUT on for the tempo and play light.
  4. For each button: go to SETTINGS > MIDI > COMMANDS, pick the command, hold LEARN and tap Send below. The Deluge learns it for this USB port.
